Agent Bus — Privacy Policy

Agent Bus is a personal automation tool with a single user: the developer who authorizes it (Katherine Akulov), using her own Google account.

  • It accesses only the Gmail account of the developer who explicitly authorized it via Google OAuth.
  • It uses that access solely to send messages, and to manage (archive/trash) threads it itself created, to that same account's own inbox.
  • It does not read, collect, transmit, sell, or share any Gmail data, message content, or account information with any third party, service, or advertiser.
  • No data from this tool is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the developer's own local automation workflow.
  • It is not distributed to, installed by, or used by anyone other than the developer.
